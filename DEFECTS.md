### Hand calculations for v2-23 … v2-28

**v2-23 — strict shrunk rate.** Pooled strict rate over every FI row in the
7-day fixture: A 20 VERIFIED of 25, B 12 of 12, C 25 of 25 → 57 / 62 =
0.9194. Shrinkage with 20 pseudo-rows: `(20 + 20 × 0.9194) / (25 + 20)` =
`38.39 / 45` = 0.8531 → 85.3. Pipeline: 85.3. ✓ (The broad figure in the
same test, `(23 + 20 × 60/62) / 45` = 0.9411 → 94.1, was already right.)

**v2-24 — v1-compatible Framing Integrity.** Eight scored rows in the
fixture. v1 counts VERIFIED, CONTEXTUAL and tone-only rows as good:
VERIFIED (row 1), tone (row 4), CONTEXTUAL (row 6), VERIFIED full text
(row 7) = 4. MISLEADING, CONTESTED, CONTESTED, CONTESTED are bad.
`4 / 8` = 50. Pipeline: 50. ✓

**v2-25 — underpowered permutation.** Minimum group size is 5. Twelve rows
of one outlet are 6 flagged + 6 rest: both ≥ 5, so the test runs. Eight rows
are 6 flagged + 2 rest: 2 < 5, underpowered. ✓

**v2-26 — one-day row sum.** Both fixture runs in the 24-hour window carry
`rows_total` 200: `200 + 200` = 400. Pipeline: 400. ✓

**v2-27 — stratified draw.** Candidates after excluding tone-only, unscored
and UNRESOLVED rows: VERIFIED 60, CONTEXTUAL 40, CONTESTED 8, UNVERIFIED 12,
MISLEADING+PROVISIONAL 5 + 9 = 14. Targets of 20 give 20, 20, 8, 12, 14 =
74. The 26 missing fill round-robin from the two strata with spare
candidates: 13 each → VERIFIED 33, CONTEXTUAL 33. Pipeline: 33 / 33 / 8 /
12 / 14. ✓

### Hand calculations for v2-1 … v2-4

Per-article v2 is `100 × F/10 × (1 − 0.25·S/10 − 0.25·C/10)`, rounded to one
decimal. Means are rounded to one decimal (`meanOf`).

**v2-1 — `computeAggregate.meanIntegrityV2`.** Rows with article text after
dedupe: `a` (latest sighting: fidelity 10, sens 2, click 2) and `b` (7, 2, 2).
`v2(10,2,2) = 100 × 1.0 × (1 − 0.05 − 0.05) = 90`.
`v2(7,2,2) = 100 × 0.7 × 0.9 = 63`. Mean `(90 + 63) / 2 = 76.5`. Pipeline: 76.5. ✓
